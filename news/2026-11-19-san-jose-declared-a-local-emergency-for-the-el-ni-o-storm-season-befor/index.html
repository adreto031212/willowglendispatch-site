San Jose declared a local emergency for the El Niño storm season before the worst of it arrives

November 19, 2026 · Willow Glen Dispatch, issue 1 · All Willow Glen and central San Jose news

The City Council proclaimed it on November 10, after new National Weather Service predictions and talks with Valley Water about flood risk along the city's creeks and rivers. It lets the city buy flood supplies and hire contractors without the usual delay, call in mutual aid and move staff to storm work. Forecasters project this season could bring the strongest winter storms on record here. The city asks residents to sign up for county alerts at AlertSCC.org and check the city's flood map.

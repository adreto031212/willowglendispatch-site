Willow Glen and central San Jose · Nov 19 to Nov 25

This week in Willow Glen and central San Jose.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 1 of the Willow Glen Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • Rain into Thursday night, then again Saturday night into Sunday. As of Wednesday afternoon the National Weather Service forecast up to three-quarters of an inch more on Thursday. VTA warns of weather delays and reroutes, and rides to or from a county warming center are free through Friday, November 20. A blocked storm drain or a flooded street goes to 311. Forecast · VTA
  • No school in San Jose Unified, Wednesday to Friday, November 25 to 27. The district's holiday recess covers all three days. SJUSD calendar

What the city decided and what changed

San Jose declared a local emergency for the El Niño storm season before the worst of it arrives

The City Council proclaimed it on November 10, after new National Weather Service predictions and talks with Valley Water about flood risk along the city's creeks and rivers.

Household batteries go out with the recycling starting January 4

Single-family homes can put alkaline AA, AAA, C, D and 9-volt batteries, plus button and coin cells, in a clear quart-size resealable bag on top of the recycling cart on collection day.

Opening a restaurant in San Jose gets a faster permit path on January 1

The city's Streamline Restaurant Program becomes the standard permit route for restaurant and food-and-beverage build-outs.

free sandbags

Valley Water hands out free filled sandbags to Santa Clara County residents from November 1 through April 15.
